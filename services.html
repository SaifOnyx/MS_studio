04. Good to know

Questions, answered.

Do you work remotely?+

Almost always. Footage moves over LucidLink or your cloud of choice, reviews happen on Frame.io with timecoded notes, and we sync over call as often as you'd like. I'm in the Cairo timezone (GMT+2) but used to overlapping with Europe and the Gulf.

What do you need to start?+

The footage (or proxies), any selects or notes you already have, brand or delivery specs, and the hard deadline. If there's a script, lookbook or reference reel, even better — the more context up front, the fewer revisions later.

How many revisions are included?+

Two rounds on a single cut, three on a campaign. Each round is a consolidated set of notes rather than rolling changes. Extra rounds are billed by the hour and always quoted before any work.

Can you edit in software we already own?+

Yes. I'm fluent in Premiere and Resolve and comfortable conforming between them. Working in the NLE your team already uses is usually cheaper and smoother than forcing a switch.

Do you do color and sound too?+

Yes — grade, conform, temp mix and sound design are all in-house. For theatrical or broadcast finishing I'll bring in a trusted colorist or re-recording mixer and supervise, so the feel stays consistent with the cut.
